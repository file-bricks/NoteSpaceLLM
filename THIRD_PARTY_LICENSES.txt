License Compatibility & Copyleft Analysis
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
NoteSpaceLLM is licensed under the GNU Affero General Public License v3.0 (AGPL-3.0).
This license structure ensures compatibility across all runtime components:

1. PyMuPDF is licensed under GNU AGPL-3.0-or-later. NoteSpaceLLM's AGPL-3.0 license
   is directly and fully compliant with PyMuPDF's copyleft requirements.
2. PySide6 (Qt for Python) is licensed under the GNU Lesser General Public License v3
   (LGPL-3.0-only). NoteSpaceLLM utilizes dynamic imports / shared libraries without
   modifying Qt source code, satisfying LGPL-3.0 section 4 requirements.
3. LangChain, ChromaDB, python-docx, openpyxl, extract-msg, pypdf, and pypdfium2
   are distributed under permissive licenses (MIT, Apache-2.0, BSD-3-Clause),
   which are fully compatible with incorporation into an AGPL-3.0 licensed application.
4. Optional cloud integrations (OpenAI, Anthropic) and packaging tools (PyInstaller
   with PyInstaller Special Exception) preserve these licensing boundaries.
