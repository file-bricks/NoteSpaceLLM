## Product Title
NoteSpace LLM Pro

## Short Description (max 100 characters)
Local AI note-taking & document analysis with RAG vector search, PDF support & research reports.

## Full Description
NoteSpaceLLM Pro is your private, local workstation for comprehensive document analysis, intelligent note-taking, and AI-powered research. Built as a privacy-focused alternative to cloud notebook services, NoteSpaceLLM keeps your documents and vector indexes stored locally on your machine.

Easily ingest PDFs, Word documents, Markdown files, and research notes to leverage state-of-the-art Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) for deep, context-aware querying across your document collections. With flexible LLM backends, you can work completely offline using local models (via Ollama) or connect to secure API workflows (Anthropic Claude, OpenAI).

Generate structured research reports, synthesized deep dives, and export your findings to Markdown, PDF, DOCX, or HTML. The included lightweight Web/PWA Companion enables mobile review workflows across tablets and smartphones without requiring any server infrastructure.

## Key Features
- Local document ingestion for PDF, DOCX, Markdown, and TXT
- Semantic RAG vector indexing and context retrieval
- Offline-first operation with local LLMs (Ollama) and cloud API support
- Structured deep research report generation and summarization
- Comprehensive export formats: Markdown, PDF, DOCX, HTML, TXT
- Offline-capable PWA Companion for on-the-go workspace reviews
- Complete privacy control: All data remains under your ownership
